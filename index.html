01 About

I’m Deendayal, a full-stack developer from Haridwar who doesn’t stop at localhost. I build the product, harden it, deploy it and keep it alive in production.

I’m in my final year of B.Tech CSE at Gurukul Kangri Vishwavidyalaya (CGPA 8.1). In the last two years I’ve delivered a live Government of Uttarakhand portal on my own, through a CERT-In empanelled security audit and onto government servers. Alongside that I did two internships, shipped freelance work for real clients and published an open-source npm package.

I care about the whole path a request takes: the Figma frame, the React component, the Express route, the MongoDB index, and the IIS rule and TLS certificate in front of them.
