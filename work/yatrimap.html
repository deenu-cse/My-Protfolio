YATRIMAP

Travel booking for the Himalayas, built end to end

Hotels, vehicle rentals and adventure activities in Uttarakhand. Plus TouristGo: hotels, cafés and shops sell trips through a QR code, and adventure companies run their whole booking desk from a phone.

I built the customer site, the Smart QR check-in system now at 15+ hotels, three admin dashboards (hotel, rental, Chardham), the TouristGo app and partner portal, and the REST APIs behind them. Real travellers book through it every day.

Test bookings, staff and admin accounts, and unpaid or cancelled bookings are left out. Activity numbers are read live from the public impact API when it’s reachable; otherwise you see the 22 Oct 2026 snapshot.

01 — TouristGo

Scan a QR, booked in under a minute, ticket on WhatsApp

A traveller scans the QR at their hotel or café and books. The adventure company sees it on its phone, scans the ticket at check-in, and WhatsApp messages go out automatically at each step.

Messages as sent by the automation · booking ID masked · swipe →
02 — Raft Pool

Rafting companies share seats in real time

  • Post “need 4 seats” or “2 seats free” for a slot
  • Requests expire in 20 minutes; offers close 10 minutes before the slot ends
  • A moved guest keeps the same booking ID and ticket
  • Seat claims are atomic, so two companies never take the same seats
03 — Payments

The server decides what was paid

Every Razorpay order is recorded with the bookings it covers. One cart payment is split across several bookings. A tampered amount or a reused payment is rejected.

04 — Where

Rishikesh to Chopta, and on to Manali

Hotels in Rishikesh, Haridwar, Mussoorie, Tehri, Chopta and Manali. Rental hubs in Rishikesh, Dehradun, Haridwar and Mussoorie. 28 activities across 6 destinations in 4 states.

07 — Engineering highlights
WhatsApp automation
Each message sent once

Approved Twilio templates go out once per guest, even when a payment callback retries. Tickets are server-rendered PDFs behind signed links, and delivery receipts return through a verified webhook.

Booking desk
Walk-ins, scans, edits

Companies take walk-ins, scan tickets, collect cash or UPI, and edit a booking’s guests, price, date or slot. Every change is logged and every ticket updates.

Privacy by default
Public numbers, private people

The impact API shares only totals, activity types and anonymous trips. Names, phone numbers, booking IDs and partner revenue never leave the server.
